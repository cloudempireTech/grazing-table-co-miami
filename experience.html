The Process

Bespoke On-Site Event Styling

From initial menu consultation to the final placement of organic edible botanicals, our team manages every detail with effortless poise.

🌿

1. Curated Consultation

We begin with an interactive discussion on WhatsApp or phone to define your guest count, color scheme, floral style, and specific dietary needs (gluten-free, vegan, nut-free).

  • ✓ Palette & moodboard alignment
  • ✓ Wine & champagne pairing guidance
  • ✓ Tailored table dimension planning
✨

2. On-Site Live Installation

Our culinary stylists arrive 2 to 3 hours before your guests enter. We lay food-grade protective parchment, arrange marble risers, and build a multi-dimensional feast.

  • ✓ Temperature-controlled transit
  • ✓ Fresh Italian foliage & citrus styling
  • ✓ Hand-carved honeycomb & charcuterie blooms
🥂

3. Effortless Celebration

Your grazing table becomes the social centerpiece of the evening. After the event, our team returns for discreet breakdown or provides eco-friendly packing for remaining items.

  • ✓ Zero host stress or kitchen cleanup
  • ✓ Bamboo tongs & eco palm leaf serveware
  • ✓ Takeaway packaging for hosts
